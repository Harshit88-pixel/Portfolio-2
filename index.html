Developer.

I'm a full-stack web developer and video editor based wherever the WiFi is good. I build polished, performant digital products and craft visual stories that move people — sometimes literally.

My philosophy is simple: every pixel and every frame should earn its place. I obsess over the details that most people don't notice — because those are the ones users feel.
